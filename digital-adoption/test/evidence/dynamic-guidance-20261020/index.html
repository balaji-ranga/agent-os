What changed

The model receives fresh form structure and validation constraints, relevant rendered DOM text/roles/states, combobox-to-option relationships, the user goal/history, authorized website/task evidence, and the preceding browser-reported action outcome. It decides whether to point, search, prepare a selection or clarify. Search-only approval triggers a new observation and a separate result-selection review. No card names, website names or framework-specific inference rules choose the model result.

Coverage is bounded loaded-page evidence: up to 60 supported fields, 30 safe links and 24 task-relevant targets. Current input values, hidden/private-marked nodes and scripts are excluded. Target refs permit highlighting only. Field execution, navigation, owner bindings and Action Policies retain their separate authority. This does not provide unrestricted browser clicking or whole-site visibility.
