The organisation is changing

AI removes busywork. Human judgement moves to the centre.

Traditional companies need many people to route tasks, collect updates, and push routine work through the system. AI-native companies flip that shape: a small human decision layer directs a broad team of specialists, while automation handles the repetitive base.
