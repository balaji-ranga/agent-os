# Cash Flow Forecast

1. Confirm forecast horizon, opening balance and evidence dates. Read approved receivables, payables, payroll and recurring obligations.
2. Model inflows and outflows by week or month; separate committed from estimated cash movements.
3. Produce base/downside scenarios with timing and probability assumptions; avoid counting invoices as both receipts and receivables.
4. State projected minimum cash and sensitivities with reconciliation gaps. This is planning, not investment advice or permission to transact.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
