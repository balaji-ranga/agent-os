# Training Plan

1. Gather role expectations, approved training policy and observed skill gaps.
2. Prioritize outcomes and design modules with prerequisites, time estimates and practice tasks.
3. Define observable assessments and manager check-ins; distinguish course completion from demonstrated proficiency.
4. Deliver a plan with resources, owner and review dates. Do not enroll staff in paid courses or publish performance judgments.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
