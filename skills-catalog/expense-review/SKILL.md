# Expense Review

1. Gather submitted receipts and the applicable policy version.
2. Check dates, claimants, category, business purpose, currency conversion, caps and duplicate claims.
3. Distinguish policy failures from missing evidence and discretionary exceptions. Never infer fraud from an anomaly alone.
4. Return documented exceptions and approver questions without approving, rejecting or reimbursing claims.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
