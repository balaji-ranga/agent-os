# Procurement Comparison

1. Gather the approved requirements, quotes and procurement policy from owner-scoped documents.
2. Normalize currency, duration, tax, implementation, support and renewal terms. Keep unknown charges separate from zero.
3. Compare functional fit, security, lock-in, contractual risk and total cost under explicit assumptions.
4. Produce a sourced shortlist and unresolved questions. Do not accept terms, buy subscriptions or send commitments.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
