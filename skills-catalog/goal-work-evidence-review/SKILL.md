# Goal Work Evidence Review

1. Locate the exact goal/run and inspect work history for its steps and timeframe.
2. Correlate tool results and deliverables to run, step and work-unit IDs. Ignore unrelated chat cards and historical results.
3. Classify each required outcome as verified, failed, pending or not observable. A successful tool call alone is not proof of the business outcome.
4. Give the evidence-supported status and precise pending checks. Do not complete, restart or cancel the goal as part of this read-only review.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
