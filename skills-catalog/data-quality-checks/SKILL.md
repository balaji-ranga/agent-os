# Data Quality Checks

1. Confirm schema, business rules, period and source lineage.
2. Check nulls, duplicates, key uniqueness, valid ranges, units, dates and cross-source totals.
3. Quantify failures and preserve representative record references without exposing sensitive raw data.
4. Deliver a reproducible validation checklist and remediation proposals. Do not rewrite or delete records without an approved change plan.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
