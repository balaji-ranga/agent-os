# CRM Account Research

1. Identify the exact account, requested qualification criteria and permitted CRM/research connections.
2. Discover suitable read actions and inspect their schemas. Retrieve existing records before proposing new information.
3. Keep public facts, CRM facts and hypotheses separate; date evidence and avoid collecting sensitive personal data.
4. Deliver a qualification brief with gaps and recommended next contact. Do not write CRM records or send outreach without approval.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
