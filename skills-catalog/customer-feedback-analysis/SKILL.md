# Customer Feedback Analysis

1. Confirm sample period, channels and permission to use feedback.
2. Remove duplicates and unnecessary personal identifiers; distinguish direct quotes from interpretation.
3. Group themes with counts, representative evidence and segment differences; disclose sampling bias.
4. Recommend testable actions and owners. Do not claim population percentages or causal effects from an unrepresentative sample.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
