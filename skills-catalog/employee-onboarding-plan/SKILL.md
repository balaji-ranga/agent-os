# Employee Onboarding Plan

1. Read the role, start date, company onboarding policy and manager requirements.
2. Plan pre-start, first-week and first-month milestones, training, equipment and check-ins.
3. List access requests by role and approval owner; apply least privilege and never provision credentials directly.
4. Deliver a dated checklist with completion evidence and open dependencies. Keep employee personal data limited to the task.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
