# Invoice Reconciliation

1. Read scoped invoices, purchase orders, receipts and payment evidence; confirm accounting period and currency.
2. Match vendor identity, invoice ID, dates, quantity, unit price, taxes and totals. Detect duplicate IDs and repeated amounts.
3. Separate matched, partially matched, missing and disputed lines with computed differences and source references.
4. Deliver an exception report for finance review. Do not approve payments, post ledger entries or change supplier banking data.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
