# Executive Briefing

1. Identify the decision, audience, deadline and supplied evidence.
2. Extract material facts and competing options; verify dates, quantities and source attribution.
3. Compare tradeoffs, costs, uncertainty and approval requirements without inventing missing evidence.
4. Deliver recommendation, reasons, risk, unresolved questions and a named next step. The brief is not authorization to execute commitments.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
