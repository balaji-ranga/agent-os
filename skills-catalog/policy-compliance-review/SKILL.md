# Policy Compliance Review

1. Identify the applicable policy version and scope from approved documents.
2. Map each process requirement to evidence, owner and policy clause.
3. Classify compliant, noncompliant, exception-needed and not-verifiable items. Avoid presenting an internal review as legal certification.
4. Return gaps and approval questions. Do not amend policy or bypass a control; professional legal/regulatory conclusions require qualified review.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
