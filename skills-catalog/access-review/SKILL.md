# Access Review

1. Gather authorized role, grant and approval evidence for the requested company scope.
2. Map access to business need, owner and expiry; detect orphaned, excessive and unapproved grants.
3. Mark incomplete inventories as gaps rather than evidence that access is absent.
4. Produce a review checklist with suggested revocations for human approval. Never grant or revoke access during this read-only review.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
