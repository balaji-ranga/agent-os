# Customer Support Triage

1. Discover the granted support read action and inspect the requested case.
2. Classify issue type, severity and customer impact using policy; prioritize safety/security incidents for the human owner.
3. Search approved knowledge before proposing steps. Separate verified fixes from hypotheses and redact unnecessary personal data.
4. Draft a response and escalation record. Do not close tickets, promise refunds or perform account changes without authorization.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
