# Recruitment Scorecard

1. Read the approved job requirements and interview evidence.
2. Define job-related criteria, anchored ratings and consistent evidence standards.
3. Score only supplied evidence; mark absent evidence as unknown and avoid protected or irrelevant personal attributes.
4. Prepare an auditable comparison and follow-up questions. A human owns hiring decisions; do not reject candidates or send messages automatically.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
