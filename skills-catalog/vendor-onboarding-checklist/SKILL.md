# Vendor Onboarding Checklist

1. Read the company's onboarding policy and supplied vendor documents.
2. Check entity identity, ownership, contact validation, banking evidence, security/privacy terms and required approvals.
3. Flag missing or inconsistent documents; never treat a vendor-provided bank change as independently verified.
4. Return a checklist with evidence locations and responsible reviewers. Do not modify payee records, transfer money or contact vendors without authorization.

## Operating boundaries

Use only current company/owner-scoped evidence and granted capabilities. Skills do not grant tools, credentials or approval. If a prerequisite is unavailable, report it exactly; never fabricate a tool result or bypass existing routing, planning, action-control or tenant isolation. Finish with evidence references, assumptions and the smallest authorized next step.
