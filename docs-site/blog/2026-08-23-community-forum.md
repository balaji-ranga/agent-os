The public discussion forum lives next to this blog. Use it for questions, ideas, and show-and-tell — not for secrets or support that needs your company data.

<!-- truncate -->

## Where to talk

Open **[Forum](https://flolah.cloud/blog/forum/)** or go straight to [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/balaji-ranga/agent-os/discussions).

Suggested starter threads:

- How you hired your first AI employee
- What you want next in Flolah
- Bugs or docs that confused you (no tenant data, no API keys)

## House rules (short)

1. Be specific and kind.
2. Never paste API keys, session tokens, or customer records.
3. Product how-to that does not need your tenant still belongs here; account-specific issues belong in the signed-in product with Platform Help.

The user guide stays at [flolah.cloud/docs/](https://flolah.cloud/docs/).
