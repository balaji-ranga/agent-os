This is the public Flolah blog. We will post product notes, how we think about an AI Company OS, and short stories from operators who run a company of AI employees.

<!-- truncate -->

## What you will find here

- **Product notes** — what shipped, why it exists, and how to use it
- **Operating ideas** — running a company with a human CEO and AI employees
- **Invites to discuss** — every post can continue in the [forum](https://flolah.cloud/blog/forum/)

## How to join the conversation

The [forum](https://flolah.cloud/blog/forum/) is hosted as **GitHub Discussions** on the public Flolah repository. Sign in with GitHub to ask a question, share a workflow, or reply to a post.

## How we publish

New posts are markdown in the public repo (`docs-site/blog/`). After a deploy they appear at [flolah.cloud/blog/](https://flolah.cloud/blog/).
