# Flolah announcements

Flolah may show a clearly labelled product or sponsored announcement after login. You can dismiss it, and eligible announcements may offer **Don't show again**. A popup impression is not treated as proof that you read it.

WhatsApp announcements are off by default. To opt in or out, open **Profile → Announcement channels**. Delivery also requires your owner-scoped WhatsApp channel to be enabled and paired. Promotional messages identify themselves as Flolah announcements and are separate from conversational COO output.

Flolah records bounded delivery and interaction evidence such as impression, viewable time, dismissal, CTA selection, and WhatsApp sent/failed status. See the [Privacy Policy](https://flolah.cloud/legal/privacy.html) for details.
