# OKR company demo pack

The Northstar Industrial Supplies demo shows how Flolah runs a company from objectives instead of isolated agent tasks.

An administrator installs a fictional Singapore distributor with:

- a CEO, two synthetic human employees, and eight AI employees;
- annual and quarterly objectives with revenue, margin, pipeline, DSO, and OTIF key results;
- seven objective-linked, multi-node workflows;
- synthetic CRM and ERP evidence plus live provider mirrors; Twenty is preferred for CRM and ERPNext Sales CRM is the safe fallback when an isolated Twenty workspace is unavailable;
- autonomous read, internal-write, and synthetic-communication policies;
- prohibited financial/destructive actions; and
- monthly token and error budgets for every AI employee.

After installation, open **Objectives** to see the FY2026 objective and active Q3 objective, **Workflows** to inspect the execution paths, **My Org** for the company structure, **Knowledge** for evidence, **Efficiency** for budgets, and **Policies** for Action Control.

The CEO can ask the COO: “Give me Q3 objective health, evidence, blockers, and next actions.” The COO delegates to the relevant employees and uses objective-linked goals and workflows. WhatsApp can be used after the CEO pairs a device; private session credentials are never included in the seed pack.

Cleanup and reseed are operator-managed and owner-confirmed. Cleanup removes only resources recorded in the pack install ledger and retains the CEO account and provider workspaces.
