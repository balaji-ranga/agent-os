# Live Operations

**Live Operations** shows what your AI company is doing now. Open it beside **Workspace** to see active agents, running and queued work, tools in use, approvals, and blockers.

## The three views

- **Live Operations** is the visual command centre. Active agents appear around the Flolah network and their current connectors appear with them. The event stream records agent work and tool activity as it happens.
- **Agent Works** groups current, queued, blocked, and idle work by AI employee. Use it when you need the exact work item behind the visual view.
- **History** is the paginated audit trail for completed and failed actions.

Only agents with current work are emphasised in the network. When the company is quiet, available agents are shown as ready instead of implying that they are executing something.

## On a phone

The network occupies its own full-width panel. Summary cards can be swiped horizontally, while Agent Works stacks into readable cards. Open the navigation drawer to move between Live Operations, Reviews, Workspace, and other company areas.

## When work needs you

An approval or policy block appears as **Needs attention**. Open its linked work item to inspect the evidence and approve, reject, or provide the missing input. The originating execution retains continuity after your decision.
