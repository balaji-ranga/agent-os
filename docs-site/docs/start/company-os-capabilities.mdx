# Company OS capability architecture

A company operating system connects the organization’s knowledge and structure to an operating engine, applies that engine across every business domain, and turns the resulting work into company outcomes.

<CapabilityArchitecture />

Green capabilities are available in Flolah today. Unfilled capabilities show the logical expansion of the Company OS; they are a capability direction, not a statement that the product currently includes those systems.

## The operating model

**Agents and Workflows are not another business module.** Together they are the operating engine across all modules:

- **Agents** own outcomes, reason from company context, delegate to specialists, use granted tools, learn, and escalate decisions.
- **Workflows** provide repeatable execution, integrations, approval gates, evidence, retries, and recovery.
- **Org and Knowledge** provide the durable company context beneath that engine.
- **Business systems** provide the systems of record through which the company sells, operates, employs, serves, plans, governs, contracts, builds, and supports itself.

## Current Flolah business capabilities

### Sell — CRM

Manage customers, leads, opportunities, proposals, and the path from a qualified deal to an ERP order. CRM Makers prepare changes and Checkers review consequential actions.

### Operate and account — ERP

Run order-to-cash and procure-to-pay, including quotes, orders, delivery, invoicing, cash, purchasing, bills, stock, accounting, and projects.

### Organize — Org

Model human and AI employees, departments, roles, reporting relationships, responsibilities, permissions, and operating budgets.

### Know — Knowledge

Maintain company documents, tables, searchable information, and durable organizational memory used by employees and workflows.

### Execute across everything — Agents and Workflows

Turn outcomes into governed action across Flolah and connected systems. Agents provide ownership and judgement; workflows provide deterministic execution and controls.

## Capability expansion

### Employ — HCM / HRIS

Recruitment, employee records, onboarding and offboarding, leave, payroll, benefits, performance, compensation, skills, learning, and succession.

### Serve — Customer Service

Customer onboarding, support cases, omnichannel service, SLAs, escalations, account health, adoption, renewals, churn management, and customer portals.

### Plan — EPM

Strategy, objectives, budgets, forecasts, scenarios, workforce and capacity planning, department plans, and actual-versus-plan management.

### Market — Marketing Operations

Audiences, campaigns, customer journeys, lead scoring, attribution, content operations, digital assets, and consent and preference management.

### Govern — GRC

Enterprise risks, controls, evidence, regulatory obligations, vendor risk, incidents, policies, compliance assessments, and business continuity.

### Contract — Legal / CLM

Contract lifecycle management, obligations, renewals, clause libraries, legal requests, approvals, signatures, corporate records, and intellectual property.

### Build — Product and Portfolio

Product strategy, discovery, roadmaps, requirements, prioritization, releases, feedback, portfolio investment, and product profitability.

### Support the company — ITSM

Service requests, incidents, problems, assets, configuration, access requests, change management, software licences, and operational incidents involving agents or tools.
