# Welcome to Flolah

**Flolah** is the **AI Company OS**. You are the CEO. You hire **AI employees**, give them roles and tools, store company knowledge, and supervise work the way you would a small team.

This public guide walks you from first access through running the company:

1. **Access** the product and create your account
2. **Set up** company identity, team, and keys
3. **Run** chat, Kanban, standups, and recurring goals
4. **Connect** workflows, CRM/ERP, and channels
5. **Operate** with approvals, budgets, and troubleshooting

You do not need APIs or infrastructure knowledge for everyday use.

## What you get on day one

After you register, Flolah provisions a starter organisation that typically includes:

- A **COO** — plans, delegates, and keeps you informed
- **Workflow Builder** — helps you design visual automations
- **Platform Help** — answers product how-to questions inside the app

You can hire more specialists, enable optional CRM/ERP, and connect WhatsApp, Slack, or business apps when you are ready.

## Two ways to get help

| Where | When to use it |
|-------|----------------|
| **This site** (`/docs`) | Open, public walkthrough — start here before or after you sign in |
| **Platform Help** (in the app) | Ask in plain language while you are logged in; it searches the same product how-to corpus |

Legal documents (Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Open source) live under [Legal](https://flolah.cloud/legal/terms.html) and in the login footer.

## What this guide does not cover

This is end-user documentation. It does not describe hosting, internal administration, or how Flolah is deployed. If something in the product needs a platform operator, the troubleshooting page tells you to wait or contact support — not how to change servers.
