# People (human employees)

The **CEO** (company owner) can invite **human employees** into the **same company**. They do not get a separate organisation or their own AI-employee grants. They work with the CEO’s AI employees, Knowledge, tools, and CRM/ERP.

Invite and roles: **My Org → People**. Place them on the chart: **My Org → Design** (drag like AI employees). Details on departments: [Org and departments](./org-and-departments.md).

## Invite

1. Open **My Org** (`/org`) → **People**.
2. Enter **name**, **email**, and optional phone.
3. Choose a **role** (CEO Delegate or Member, or a custom role).
4. Flolah emails a **password-reset / invite** link (valid several days).
5. They sign in at the same login page as you.

Only the CEO and a **CEO Delegate** can invite, disable, or change roles.

## Roles

| Kind | Access |
|------|--------|
| **CEO (you)** | Full company. Platform Admin stays with Flolah operators. |
| **CEO Delegate** | Same company screens as the CEO (org, tools, people). Not platform Admin. Cannot remove the CEO account. |
| **Member** | Always: **Home**, **Kanban**, **Profile**. Extra screens only if you tick them (My Org, Efficiency, CRM, ERP, Knowledge, …). |

CRM/ERP ticks mean they may **open** those desks. They sign in with **their email** inside the **company** CRM/ERP workspace — not a separate Twenty or ERPNext company.

## Always on

Every employee can open Home (COO chat), Kanban, and Profile. Members chat and delegate with the **COO** and **AI employees in their department**. CEO and CEO Delegate see the full team.

## Kanban

Everyone **sees all cards**. Department members may **act, chat, approve, or reopen** only when the assignee (AI employee or person) is in **their department**. Unassigned cards are view-only for members. CEO and CEO Delegate can act on any card.

## Efficiency

**Efficiency View → Department** includes people task counts. **User View** (`/efficiency?tab=user`) is Kanban performance for people this month (parallel to Agent View).

## COO

Treat people as employees. Prefer **AI employees** for execution; send **approvals** to humans. If no specialist fits, the COO can assign a Kanban card to a person.
