# Company knowledge

Path: **Knowledge** (`/master-data`).

This is the company memory AI employees can search: **tables** (rows and columns) and **documents** (files for search).

## Tables

Your account starts with a **departments** table. Add or edit departments here; they appear when you assign employees.

Other tables appear as you use the product (for example company memory after Update Company Details, or discovery lists from Business Discovery).

You can import **CSV** where the UI offers it.

## Documents

Upload **PDF, Word, Excel, or text**. Content is extracted so employees can answer from your policies and handbooks.

Ask in chat: “list departments”, “what does our PTO policy say?”

**Inbound attachments** from chat paperclip and from WhatsApp/Slack land here (and under the employee’s inbound folder) so they can be searched or transcribed.

## What you will not see in Documents

Flolah’s **product help** is indexed for Platform Help and specialists. It is **not** listed as your upload, and you cannot delete it. **Purge all uploads** only removes **your** files.

## Content Explorer

**Content Explorer** (`/content-explorer`) browses uploaded and generated files (preview/download/delete). Use it to reclaim storage; **Efficiency View → Org → Storage (MB)** shows the effect.
