# Update company details

Path: avatar → **Update Company Details** (`/update-company-details`).

Use this when the company **name, mission, DNA, or industry** should change **without** re-running the full Company setup wizard (no new team pack).

Edits are stored in Knowledge (company memory) and kept in sync with your setup profile. Full team Apply remains under [Company setup](./company-setup.md).

**How we run** / Company Operate (`/company-operate`) is a separate “how this company operates” surface for content and process narratives — not a replacement for this form.
