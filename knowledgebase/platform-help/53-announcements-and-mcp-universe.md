# Announcements and MCP Universe

## MCP Universe

[MCP Universe](https://flolah.cloud/free-tools/mcp-universe/) is Flolah's free public directory of Model Context Protocol servers. It is a discovery catalog, not a connection to your company.

1. Complete the human check, then search or filter listings.
2. Open a listing to inspect publisher, source repository, documentation, transport, declared authentication, license, version, capabilities, and provenance.
3. Use **Include your MCP** to submit a listing for moderation; registering in the official MCP Registry first is preferred.
4. To use a server, review it independently and configure it under **Connectors → MCPs** or the MCP registry. Merely appearing in MCP Universe does not grant it access, execute its tools, or install it for an employee.

Listings come from configured authoritative registries or approved community submissions. Flolah retains the stated source and does not invent missing publisher metadata. Discovery does not execute advertised MCP tools.

## Product and sponsored announcements

Flolah may show a clearly labelled product or sponsored announcement after login. You can dismiss it; eligible announcements may also offer **Don't show again**. An impression alone is not treated as evidence that you read the content.

WhatsApp announcements are off by default. Use **Profile → Announcement channels** to opt in or out. WhatsApp delivery also requires the owner's channel to be enabled and paired. These notices identify themselves as Flolah announcements and remain separate from conversational COO or employee output.

Flolah records bounded delivery/interaction evidence such as impression, viewable time, dismissal, CTA selection, and WhatsApp sent/failed status. See the public Privacy Policy for the current data description.

## Admin controls

Platform admins manage the moderated MCP Universe catalog and announcement campaigns from their Admin navigation. Publication and promotion records are platform operations; they do not expose a CEO's Master Data, agent chats, connector credentials, or company workspace.
