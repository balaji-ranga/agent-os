# Flolah — One-Pager Pitch

**Automate. Innovate. Elevate.**

**Flolah is the operating system for an AI agent company**—where a solo founder can run named agents like a real team, so a one-person company no longer feels like one person.

Then publish those capabilities as secure, callable services.

---

## The problem

Today's AI tooling forces a false choice:

- **Chat tools** give you clever assistants with no org, no accountability, and no way to productize work.
- **Workflow canvases** automate systems but treat agents as anonymous steps—no identity, memory, or company structure.
- **Raw APIs and open webhooks** ship capability without deny-by-default access, audit, or multi-tenant isolation.

Solo founders and lean operators still feel like a one-person shop: every handoff lives in their head, every specialist is themselves. Founders who want to *run a company of agents*—roles, delegation, knowledge, human oversight, and partner-ready services—have no single home base.

---

## The solution

**Flolah** is the runtime and product surface for agentic work. You stay the CEO; the company gains an org that works while you sleep.

| What CEOs get | How it works |
|---------------|--------------|
| **An org of specialists** | COO + domain agents with durable identity (`SOUL`, `ORG`, `AGENTS`, `TOOLS`, memory) |
| **Plain-language direction** | Chat, broadcast, standups—progress on Kanban and in the notification bell |
| **A teachable company** | Master Data tables + searchable documents (including product help agents can RAG) |
| **Composable automation** | Visual workflows that call agents, tools, MCP, APIs, and CEO approvals |
| **Productized agents** | Publish a workflow as an **A2A agent** on AgentExchange—discoverable, sync/async, policy-gated |
| **Tenant isolation** | One CEO's agents, data, secrets, and standups never leak into another's company |

**One line:** *With Flolah, a one-person company doesn't feel like one person anymore—and those agents ship as secure, protocol-native services.*

---

## Why Flolah wins

1. **Agent-native org, not flow-only automation** — The unit of value is a named agent with purpose, peers, and tool grants—not an anonymous node in a graph. Delegation feels like staffing a company, not poking a chatbot.
2. **Human control plane** — Notifications, standups, approvals, and Platform Help keep the CEO in the loop without forcing every action through a builder.
3. **Governed AgentExchange (A2A)** — Deny-by-default network policy, optional OAuth, sync or async invoke (enquire + callback), owner test path, and admin-visible invocation history—including blocks that never start a run.
4. **Multi-tenant by design** — Isolation and entitlements span workspaces, APIs, schedulers, Master Data, and A2A ownership.

## Who it's for

**Solo founders and lean operators** who want a company that feels staffed—not only automation builders wiring integrations. Stand up, run, and monetize (or share) a company of AI agents while you remain the human in charge.

## Success story (in one journey)

A CEO provisions specialists → asks the COO to get work done → sees it on Kanban → teaches the company from Master Data → automates a repeatable capability in Workflows → **publishes it on AgentExchange under Deny all** → verifies with Test agent → opens access for a partner IP or OAuth client → an admin can later see every blocked or successful call.

The metaphor stays clear: *a company of agents*—not a pile of anonymous jobs. One person at the top; a full org underneath.
