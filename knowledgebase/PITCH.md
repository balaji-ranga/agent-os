## The problem

Today's AI tooling forces a false choice:

- **Chat tools** give you clever assistants with no org, no accountability, and no way to productize work.
- **Workflow canvases** automate systems but treat workers as anonymous steps—no identity, memory, or company structure.
- **Raw APIs and open webhooks** ship capability without deny-by-default access, audit, or multi-tenant isolation.

Crowded categories—AI agent platforms, agent builders, workflow automation—optimize for graphs and prompts. Solo founders still feel like a one-person shop: every handoff lives in their head. Founders who want to *run an AI company*—roles, delegation, knowledge, human oversight, and partner-ready services—have no single home base.

---

## The solution

**Flolah** is the runtime and product surface for an AI-native company. You stay the CEO; the company gains digital employees that work while you sleep.

| What CEOs get | How it works |
|---------------|--------------|
| **An org of AI employees** | COO + specialists with durable identity (`SOUL`, `ORG`, workspace docs, memory, tools) |
| **Plain-language direction** | Chat, broadcast, standups—progress on Kanban and in the notification bell |
| **Company knowledge** | Master Data tables + searchable documents (including product help for RAG) |
| **Composable automation** | Visual workflows that call employees, tools, MCP, APIs, and CEO approvals |
| **Productized services** | Publish a workflow as an **A2A agent** on AgentExchange—discoverable, sync/async, policy-gated |
| **Tenant isolation** | One CEO’s employees, data, secrets, and standups never leak into another’s company |

**One line:** *With Flolah, a one-person company doesn't feel like one person anymore—and those AI employees ship as secure, protocol-native services.*

---

## Why Flolah wins

1. **Company-native, not flow-only** — The unit of value is a named AI employee with purpose, peers, and tool grants—not an anonymous node in a graph.
2. **Human control plane** — Notifications, standups, approvals, policies, and Platform Help keep the CEO in the loop without forcing every action through a builder.
3. **Governed AgentExchange (A2A)** — Deny-by-default network policy, optional OAuth, sync or async invoke (enquire + callback), owner test path, and admin-visible invocation history.
4. **Multi-tenant by design** — Isolation and entitlements span workspaces, APIs, schedulers, knowledge, and A2A ownership.
5. **OS primitives** — People, Employees, Departments, Knowledge, Tools, Policies, Workflows, Tasks, Approvals, Memory—so the product stays coherent as it grows.

## Success story (in one journey)

A CEO hires specialists → asks the COO to get work done → sees it on Kanban → teaches the company from Knowledge (Master Data) → automates a repeatable capability in Workflows → **publishes on AgentExchange under Deny all** → verifies with Test agent → opens access for a partner → an admin can later see every blocked or successful call.

The metaphor stays clear: *an AI company*—not a pile of anonymous jobs. One person at the top; a full org underneath.
