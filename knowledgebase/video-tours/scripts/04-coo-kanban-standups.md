## Voice script (draft)

Treat the COO as your chief of staff. Delegate specialty work; watch Kanban for agent-owned status. Standups roll team progress back to the COO. Broadcast reaches many agents with pacing. Use notify when you need a deliberate CEO alert — not for every token of chatter. This loop is daily adoption.

## Shot list

1. COO chat delegate
2. Kanban board
3. Standups
4. Broadcast entry
5. Notification bell
