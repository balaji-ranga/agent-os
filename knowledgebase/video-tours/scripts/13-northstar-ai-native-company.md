# Northstar Industrial — operating as an AI-native company with Flolah

Featured live-product demonstration. Target duration: 5–6 minutes. The recording starts as **Maya Tan, CEO** and never shows the Admin interface or an impersonation banner.

All company, people, customer, supplier, opportunity, invoice, objective, and performance data shown in this demonstration is synthetic seed data.

## Scene 1 — One operating context

**Route:** Home (`/`), then Knowledge (`/master-data`)

### Voiceover

Meet Northstar Industrial, a growing industrial technology company. Maya Tan, the CEO, wants higher sales, lower operating cost, and faster execution—but without creating another disconnected collection of AI tools. Northstar uses Flolah as a single operating layer. Company context, objectives, agents, workflows, business systems, policies, budgets, and channels stay connected. This is Maya's normal user view. She can see the operating pulse of the company immediately, while the shared Knowledge area gives every authorised agent the same governed business context.

## Scene 2 — A visible human and AI organisation

**Route:** My Org (`/org`)

### Voiceover

Northstar's organisation is designed as one team of people and AI workers. Maya and her operations lead remain accountable. Specialist agents cover sales development, revenue operations, customer success, procurement, finance, and operating coordination. Each agent has a clear role, an owner, permitted tools, and an explicit budget. This avoids the common AI journey where separate teams buy separate assistants, duplicate work, and lose accountability. In Flolah, delegation follows the organisation, and the CEO can see who—or what—is responsible for each outcome.

## Scene 3 — Objectives become measurable work

**Route:** Objectives (`/objectives`)

### Voiceover

Northstar's yearly direction is translated into quarterly objectives and measurable key results. The leadership team can see targets for qualified pipeline, conversion, customer retention, procurement savings, and operating efficiency in one place. Objectives are not presentation slides that disappear after a review meeting. Goals and work are linked back to the measure they are expected to move. Progress can be updated from business events, CRM and ERP records, workflow results, and agent reports. When a measure drifts, the company can respond while there is still time to change the outcome.

## Scene 4 — Workflows turn intent into repeatable execution

**Route:** Workflows (`/workflows`)

### Voiceover

Objectives become repeatable execution through workflows. Northstar has flows for lead qualification, opportunity follow-up, customer risk, procurement review, invoice follow-up, and the executive operating rhythm. A workflow is more than a scheduled automation: it gives agents a governed sequence of events, decisions, actions, and evidence. The same process can run consistently every day, while exceptions are visible. Teams can improve the workflow once and let every future run benefit, instead of relying on tribal knowledge or one-off prompts.

## Scene 5 — Revenue execution stays connected to CRM

**Route:** CRM (`/crm`)

### Voiceover

For sales growth, Northstar's CRM provides the customer and opportunity system of record. The seed scenario includes accounts, contacts, and an active opportunity pipeline. Sales agents can prioritise follow-up, prepare account context, and report pipeline movement against the quarterly objective. CRM data stays connected to the operating model, rather than being copied into private spreadsheets or chat threads. Maya can move from the objective to the underlying commercial activity and see whether the company is building enough qualified pipeline to reach the revenue target.

## Scene 6 — Cost and fulfilment connect to ERP

**Route:** ERP (`/erp`)

### Voiceover

Northstar's ERP view connects suppliers, items, invoices, and operational records to the same company context. Procurement and finance agents can monitor supplier decisions, cost-saving opportunities, and invoice follow-up while preserving the ERP as the transactional source of truth. This is how Northstar reduces cost without losing control: agents analyse and coordinate the work, workflows standardise it, and the business system retains the authoritative record. The CEO sees commercial growth and operational efficiency as two sides of the same plan.

## Scene 7 — Autonomy is governed by policy

**Route:** Policies (`/policies`)

### Voiceover

Northstar is configured for end-to-end autonomous operation where the company has decided autonomy is appropriate. Read, internal-write, and approved external communication actions can proceed without routine human approval. High-impact financial or destructive actions remain prohibited. These rules are centrally visible and apply across agents and workflows. Autonomy therefore does not mean removing control. It means deciding the control model once, making it explicit, and allowing routine work to move quickly inside those boundaries.

## Scene 8 — Every agent has an economic envelope

**Route:** Efficiency (`/efficiency`)

### Voiceover

AI work must also be economically accountable. Northstar assigns token and operating budgets by agent, then measures usage, errors, and efficiency. The company can invest more in agents that move important key results and investigate agents that consume resources without producing enough value. This connects AI cost to operating outcomes. Instead of discovering an uncontrolled bill at month end, Maya has a live view of the capacity allocated across her digital workforce.

## Scene 9 — The CEO operates through one channel

**Route:** Channels (`/channels`), then Home (`/`)

### Voiceover

Maya does not need to visit every screen to run the company. Flolah can channel requests, exceptions, summaries, and progress updates through the COO agent and connected channels such as WhatsApp. A request can be routed to the right agent, executed through the right workflow, checked against policy and budget, and reported back with evidence. The UI remains available for inspection and control, while the day-to-day executive experience is simple: ask, review the outcome, and intervene only when the system surfaces a real exception.

## Scene 10 — One system, compounding improvement

**Route:** Objectives (`/objectives`), then Home (`/`)

### Voiceover

Without this operating layer, Northstar's AI journey would fragment across chat tools, spreadsheets, CRM automations, ERP scripts, and personal assistants. Context would drift, ownership would blur, and every integration would become a new island. With Flolah, objectives provide direction; agents provide specialised capacity; workflows provide repeatability; CRM and ERP provide business truth; and policies and budgets provide control. The result is a company that can pursue sales growth, cost reduction, and operating efficiency as one coordinated system—and improve that system every quarter.

## Production notes

- Capture the deployed Northstar seed tenant with a temporary normal CEO session; never record the Admin UI.
- Read-only navigation only. Do not run workflows, send messages, approve actions, or change records during capture.
- Record at 1920×1080, H.264 video, AAC audio, and provide WebVTT captions.
- Use a warm, confident executive voice at a measured pace.
- Keep the synthetic-data disclosure in the opening title or description.
